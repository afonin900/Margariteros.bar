# Stage 6 — Textures / Backgrounds

Master direction: **Lime Fiesta**.

## Production rule

All production masters were created natively at **1536×1536 px**. No source image was enlarged and no fragment of the brand-board was used as a crop source. The five backgrounds are repeatable tiles, so a larger canvas should be filled by repetition rather than by stretching one bitmap.

## Background masters

- `lf_bg_black_grunge.png` — primary dark poster/video background;
- `lf_bg_cream_paper.png` — light menu and editorial background;
- `lf_bg_lime_paint.png` — strong promotional background;
- `lf_bg_orange_paint.png` — secondary high-energy background;
- `lf_bg_black_chalk.png` — softer charcoal alternative.

## Transparent overlays

- `lf_overlay_grain_dark.png` and `lf_overlay_grain_light.png` — subtle print grain;
- `lf_overlay_distress_dark.png` and `lf_overlay_distress_light.png` — stronger wear/scratch layer;
- `lf_overlay_vignette.png` — edge focus for posters and video;
- `lf_overlay_glow_lime.png` and `lf_overlay_glow_orange.png` — controlled central glow.

Recommended starting opacity: grain **8–18%**, distress **15–35%**, vignette **20–45%**, glow **20–55%**. Use no more than two overlay effects at once unless the composition is intentionally maximalist.

## Quality and file-size policy

- native detail only; no fake 4000–6000 px exports;
- PNG remains the delivery format;
- palette optimization is used only where it does not visibly damage texture;
- full production package is intended to stay compact enough for everyday use;
- `lf_texture_seam_test.png` demonstrates a 2×2 repeat.
