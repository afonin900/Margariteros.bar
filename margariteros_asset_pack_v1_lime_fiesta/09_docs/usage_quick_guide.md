# Lime Fiesta — Quick Usage Guide

## Fast recipe for a poster

1. Choose black or cream texture from `06_textures_backgrounds/`.
2. Add one `papel picado` strip at the top.
3. Place the primary logo with clear space.
4. Add one large lime or cream headline.
5. Put date/time in orange or on an orange paint stroke.
6. Add one mascot pose or one icon cluster.
7. Finish with a short CTA and check contrast at phone size.

## Fast recipe for Reels

Use a blank or overlay file from `08_video_social_templates_pack/`, follow the safe-zone guide, keep text to one short message per scene, and end with the CTA card.

## Mascot lock

One stem, one base, no legs. Hands may move; sombrero construction must stay consistent.

## Scaling

- Logos, icons and mascots may be reduced.
- Do not enlarge beyond native dimensions.
- Seamless backgrounds and patterns should be repeated, not stretched.

## Color balance

Aim for approximately 60% black or cream, 30% lime and no more than 10% orange accent.
